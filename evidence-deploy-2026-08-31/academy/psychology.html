The psychology of trading

Why traders who know exactly what to do still do something else — and what actually helps.

Foundation9 chapters · 27 min · the whole curriculum

Loss aversion, tilt, revenge trading, sizing as a psychological decision, what a win rate does and does not tell you, and how to design a rule you will not break.

Chapters

1 Knowing is not doing
3 min · The gap is between calm-you writing the plan and stressed-you applying it.
2 Losses hurt more than wins feel good
3 min · Loss aversion means a loss registers more strongly than an equivalent gain (Kahneman & Tversky).
3 Tilt: how it starts and what it looks like
3 min · Tilt is a state, not a mood: still trading, no longer trading your method.
4 Revenge trading and the cost of immediacy
3 min · The test: would you take this trade if the last one had won?
5 Position size is a psychological decision
3 min · Losing streaks are certain; your state during them is what size controls.
6 Judging a trade you lost
3 min · Decision quality and outcome are different things and often disagree.
7 Win rate tells you almost nothing
3 min · Win rate says nothing without the average win and average loss beside it.
8 Losing streaks are not a signal
3 min · Long losing runs are an ordinary property of any method with losses.
9 Designing rules you will not break
3 min · Unenforceable rules depend on judgement in the moment judgement fails — replace with countable ones.
