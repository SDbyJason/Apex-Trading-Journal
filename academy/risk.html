Risk and position size

The part of trading that is arithmetic rather than opinion — and the only part you control completely.

Foundation6 chapters · 40 min · the whole curriculum

What risk actually is, how to size a position backwards from it, why a fixed percentage behaves so differently from a fixed lot, and how a daily limit stops a bad day becoming a bad month.

Chapters

1 Risk is an amount, not a lot size
6 min · Risk = stop distance × value per unit × size. Only size is freely yours.
2 Sizing backwards from the loss you accept
7 min · Size = accepted loss ÷ stop distance ÷ value per unit. Compute it before the ticket.
3 What a losing run actually does to an account
8 min · Losing runs are expected behaviour, not evidence the method broke.
4 The stop belongs to the idea
6 min · The stop marks where the idea is wrong, not what you can afford.
5 The daily limit and the stop-trading rule
6 min · Per-trade risk controls one outcome; a daily limit controls the sequence.
6 Prop accounts: someone else’s rules, the same arithmetic
7 min · Prop rules are the same three constraints with harder consequences.
