How the drawdown types work

Trailing (intraday): the fail line moves up with your highest balance, including open profit during the day. A winning trade you give back still moves the line.

Trailing (end of day): the fail line only moves up with your closing balance at the end of each day.

Static: the fail line stays at account size minus max drawdown and never moves.

Many firms stop trailing once the line reaches a set level (often the starting balance). Enter that level in "Drawdown stops trailing at".
