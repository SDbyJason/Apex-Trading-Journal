When Edgewonk is the better choice

Edgewonk is the better choice if you want one flat yearly price with nothing gated, and its Tiltmeter and trade-management optimizer are exactly the tools you are looking for.

When Evidence is the better choice

Evidence is the stronger choice if you also want to practise — replay a past day, test a strategy — and want the AI to answer when you ask rather than once a week. You can also pay monthly, or stay on the free plan.

Switching from Edgewonk

Export your trades from Edgewonk as a CSV file and drop it into Evidence’s importer — there is a preset for Edgewonk’s export, so symbol, direction, prices, size, P&L, setup and notes are mapped for you. The import check shows duplicates and missing stops before anything is written. Screenshots do not come along, because the export file does not contain them. Importing is free on every plan — no trial and no card needed.
