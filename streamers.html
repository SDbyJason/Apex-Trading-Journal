Your trading, live on stream — measured, not claimed

Viewers have seen enough screenshots of green days. An overlay that reads straight from your journal shows every trade as it closes — the losers included. That is what makes it believable.

Live examples with sample numbers — the same page you put into OBS.

What the overlay shows

  • Today in R, money or pips — your choice, and money can be switched off entirely.
  • Trades, win rate and green-day streak, plus the last 7 days.
  • The position you are in — symbol and direction, nothing more.
  • A flash when a trade closes, green or red, with the result.
  • Your journal link and referral code — optional.

Three layouts: a card in any corner, a bar along the bottom of the stream, and a poster for breaks and the intro. It updates by itself every 20 seconds from your Evidence journal — trades from cTrader, MT5, TopstepX, Binance and Bybit arrive there automatically.

Set up in two minutes

  1. In Evidence: Settings → Connect → Stream overlay. Pick the layout and what to show.
  2. Copy the link.
  3. In OBS or Streamlabs: Sources → + → Browser, paste it, width 1920, height 1080. The background is transparent.

The link carries an overlay key, not your API key. It can read the overlay numbers and nothing else — not your notes, not your screenshots, not your other trades. If it ever shows up on stream, “New link” replaces it in one click.
