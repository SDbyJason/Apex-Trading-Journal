Set once, measured everywhere

Pick MyFundedFutures, the programme, your account size and the phase in the Prop mode — five short steps. From then on the whole app measures that account with these rules: a bar under the navigation with the distance to your max loss in dollars and the room left today, the challenge on top of the overview, a warning on the trade card when a stop would use most of today’s room, and a plain message when every condition is met or which rule broke on which day.

MyFundedFutures’ trading day ends at 5 pm New York time. Evidence counts the day — and the consistency share — on that clock.

Getting the trades in

MyFundedFutures accounts run on Tradovate and NinjaTrader. Evidence has no direct Tradovate connection yet: your trades come in through a statement import or live through the NinjaTrader tracker (beta).

What a journal adds to the rule numbers

  • Edge & Leaks — every symbol, session, hour and habit tested against your other trades, so you see what passes challenges for you and what quietly fails them.
  • A cost ledger for fees, resets and payouts, so you know what the account really cost.
  • Guardrails — a personal stop below MyFundedFutures’s daily limit, a trade cap and a cooldown after a loss. Free.

When you do not need Evidence

If you want trades to arrive without any import, a journal with a Tradovate connection fits better today. Evidence does not have one yet.
